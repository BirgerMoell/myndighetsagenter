  decisions by Swedish authorities are not protected by copyright (upphovsrättslagen 9 §).
- Regleringsbrev come from [PierreMesure/oppna-statsliggaren](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PierreMesure/oppna-statsliggaren) (CC0).

  published by those authorities. The licence above covers the selection, structuring and labelling,
  not the authorities' own text.
